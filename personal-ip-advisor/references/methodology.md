# Methodology

## IP-GROW Thinking

Use this mental model for most self-media strategy work:

1. Insight: identify the person's real traits, professional assets, audience tension, and market gap.
2. Positioning: define who they help, in what situation, with what method, toward what result.
3. Content: design repeatable pillars, series, hooks, scripts, and proof formats.
4. Relationship: build trust through personality, case evidence, consistency, and interaction.
5. Offer: connect content to private messages, diagnosis, consultation, lead magnets, events, or sales.
6. Workflow: turn execution into daily/weekly/monthly SOP.

## Product To Purchase Reason

Do not stop at product features. Translate product/service into:

- Scenario: when does the audience need this?
- Cost: what happens if they ignore it?
- Belief shift: what wrong belief must change?
- Emotional benefit: what fear is reduced or desire is approached?
- Trust proof: why this person/brand can solve it?
- Action: what should the audience do next?

## Pain Point To Root Cause

Weak content says: "You have this pain."

Strong content says: "You think this pain comes from A, but the true reason is B. Here is the model that fixes it."

Template:

1. Pain/contradiction: "很多人以為..."
2. Root cause: "但真正卡住你的不是..., 而是..."
3. Model: "你要先看三件事..."
4. Case: "我看過一個客戶..."
5. Action: "先做這一步..."

## Persona Matching

Do not force every client into a loud influencer persona.

Common persona types:

- Trusted professional: calm, clear, reliable, good for insurance, finance, medical, legal-like fields.
- Warm companion: approachable, life-oriented, good for relationship-heavy service providers.
- Sharp strategist: direct, contrarian, good for consultants and business owners.
- Field operator: hands-on, case-driven, good for sales, stores, offline services.
- Mentor/coach: training-focused, good for managers, recruiters, and team leaders.
- Lifestyle expert: taste, aesthetic, routine, good for beauty, wellness, retail, travel.

## Boss IP Three Stages

Use for founders, managers, business owners, agency leaders, and recruiting leaders.

1. Exploration stage:
   - Owner appears personally.
   - Test persona, topics, audience response, and lead quality.
   - Goal: find validated topics and trust angles.

2. Replication stage:
   - Turn winning topics, scripts, shooting style, editing, DM handling, and sales talk into SOP.
   - Train team/KOS accounts to echo the owner IP.
   - Goal: scale from individual output to team content system.

3. Leadership stage:
   - Owner IP becomes market narrative and company trust asset.
   - Connect official account, team accounts, sales, events, private domain, and partner channels.
   - Goal: create category authority and conversion leverage.

## Nurturing Short-Video Series

Use "養成系口播" when a client needs long-term trust rather than one-off viral hits.

Four layers:

1. Persona nurturing: from teacher to companion.
2. Narrative nurturing: every video belongs to a larger journey.
3. Value nurturing: each video gives a piece of the system, not the whole system.
4. Emotion nurturing: the audience feels they are progressing with the creator.

Series examples:

- 30 days to understand your insurance policy.
- 90 days for a new insurance agent to build trust.
- A manager's weekly training notes.
- One store owner's customer-growth diary.

## Offline Activity As Content Asset

Turn offline scenes into content:

- Training: one learner problem, one mentor answer.
- Client meeting: one common misunderstanding.
- Team meeting: one management principle.
- Event: one trend, one case, one CTA.
- After-sales/service: one trust-building moment.

One offline event can become:

- 1 teaser video.
- 3 key-point videos.
- 3 Q&A videos.
- 2 case breakdowns.
- 1 long-form recap.
- 1 carousel/checklist.
- 1 livestream topic.
- 1 lead magnet.
