# Client Intake

Use when the client has no existing account or has not started short video.

## Intake Sections

Collect:

1. Basic identity:
   - Name, age range, location, profession, company/brand.

2. Business goal:
   - More clients, recruiting, sales, awareness, personal influence, offline traffic.

3. Offer:
   - Product/service, price range, sales process, target location, delivery method.

4. Audience:
   - Current customers, ideal customers, people they do not want to attract.

5. Personal story:
   - Career path, turning points, why they entered the field, values.

6. Strengths:
   - What others praise, what people ask them for, unique skills, proof/cases.

7. Personality:
   - Communication style, publicness boundary, comfort on camera, humor/emotion level.

8. Content resources:
   - Existing photos/videos, testimonials, cases, offline scenes, team, events.

9. Constraints:
   - Time, compliance, privacy, brand rules, filming ability, editing support.

10. Success metrics:
   - Followers, DMs, appointments, signups, sales, recruits, store visits.

## Starter Questionnaire

Ask the client:

1. What do you want people to think of when they hear your name?
2. Who do you most want to attract in the next 90 days?
3. What problem can you solve better than most people around you?
4. What do customers usually misunderstand before working with you?
5. What are 3 real cases you can talk about without exposing private data?
6. What topics can you repeat for one year without getting bored?
7. What style do you reject: too salesy, too funny, too sharp, too emotional, too professional?
8. How many videos can you realistically produce weekly?
9. What should viewers do after they trust you?
10. What proof can we show in the first month?

## Zero-To-One Output

After intake, produce:

- IP positioning.
- Audience definition.
- Content pillars.
- 30-day starter topic bank.
- First 3 pinned scripts.
- Weekly filming plan.
- DM and lead magnet plan.
- 90-day growth roadmap.
