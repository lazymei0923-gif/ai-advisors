# Script And Copywriting

## Rewrite Principles

When optimizing a script:

1. Preserve the creator's identity and intent.
2. Strengthen the first 3 seconds.
3. Replace broad claims with concrete stakes.
4. Move from pain point to root cause.
5. Use one central idea per video.
6. Add examples, numbers, or scenes when available.
7. End with a natural CTA tied to the topic.

## Viral Potential Checklist

Assess:

- Hook: is there tension, curiosity, or contradiction?
- Audience: does the target viewer know "this is about me"?
- Conflict: is there a belief being challenged?
- Specificity: are there numbers, scenes, roles, or examples?
- Trust: does the speaker sound experienced rather than salesy?
- Retention: is there a reason to keep watching?
- CTA: does it invite a concrete next action?

## 90-Second Oral Script Structure

Use:

1. 0-3s hook: contrarian statement or high-stakes question.
2. 3-10s promise: what the viewer will understand after watching.
3. 10-25s familiar scene: show the viewer's real situation.
4. 25-55s root cause: explain the hidden logic.
5. 55-75s method/case: give a model or concrete example.
6. 75-90s conclusion/CTA: summarize and guide next action.

## Hook Types

- Counter-belief: "你以為 A，其實真正關鍵是 B。"
- Cost warning: "這件事你現在不處理，未來會用更貴的方式補課。"
- Identity callout: "如果你是 30-45 歲家庭主責者，這支影片要先存起來。"
- Story opening: "以前的我，其實也很討厭保險。"
- Mistake list: "很多新人做不起來，不是因為不努力，而是犯了這三個錯。"

## Case Breakdown Template

```
今天這個案例，不是要告訴你他有多厲害，
而是要拆給你看，他真正做對了哪三件事。

第一，他沒有先賣產品，而是先讓客戶看見問題。
第二，他把複雜選擇變成簡單判斷標準。
第三，他留下了一個低壓的下一步。

所以你要學的不是表面的話術，
而是他背後的信任順序。
```

## Insurance Script Upgrade

Avoid:

- Too many product terms.
- Too much compensation detail without compliance framing.
- Fear-only selling.
- Overpromising income or claim outcomes.

Prefer:

- Risk sequence.
- Family decision scenes.
- Policy review logic.
- Claim story with learning.
- Career asset logic with disclaimer.
- "This is not to say insurance is easy/rich, but to show the business structure and responsibility."

## CTA Templates

- "如果你想知道自己卡在流量、信任還是成交，私訊我『診斷』。"
- "想要這份檢查表，留言/私訊『清單』。"
- "如果你也遇到類似狀況，把你的問題丟給我，我用下一支影片拆。"
- "先收藏，下一集我會講完整流程。"
