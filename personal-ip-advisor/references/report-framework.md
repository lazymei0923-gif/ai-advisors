# Full Report Framework

Use this for deliverable account-diagnosis reports.

## 1. Executive Summary

Include:

- Account type and current business objective.
- Current positioning in one sentence.
- Main opportunity.
- Main bottleneck.
- Recommended IP direction.
- 90-day target.

## 2. Public Account Observation

Separate platform observations:

- FB: profile, public posts, reels, interaction, relationship signals, life/professional mix.
- IG: bio, reels, highlights, pinned posts, visual style, CTA, engagement.
- Website/store if available: offer clarity, product trust, path to contact/purchase.

State access limitations if only partial public content is visible.

## 3. Personality And Strength Inference

Infer from visible content and user-provided context:

- Personality traits.
- Communication style.
- Interests and preferences.
- Professional strengths.
- Relationship style.
- Credibility assets.
- Potential content advantages.
- Risks or unsuitable directions.

## 4. Current Account Diagnosis

Assess five dimensions:

1. IP clarity: can a stranger know who this person is?
2. Audience clarity: who is the account speaking to?
3. Content structure: are there repeatable pillars and series?
4. Trust building: are proof, cases, values, and relationship assets visible?
5. Conversion path: does the audience know what to do next?

## 5. IP Positioning

Provide:

- One-line positioning.
- 3-5 persona labels.
- Persona promise.
- Avoided persona traps.
- Bio rewrite.
- Pinned-post/reel strategy.

Positioning format:

`I help [specific audience] solve [high-cost problem] through [method/strength] so they can [result].`

## 6. Target Audience

For each audience segment:

- Who they are.
- Situation.
- Pain.
- Hidden desire.
- Objections.
- Best content angle.
- Best CTA.

## 7. Content Strategy

Build 4-6 pillars:

- Awareness/education.
- Case/proof.
- Persona/lifestyle.
- Behind-the-scenes/process.
- Objection handling.
- Conversion/offer.

For each pillar include:

- Purpose.
- Example topics.
- Suggested format.
- CTA.

## 8. Topic Bank

Provide 30-60 topics when writing a complete report.

Topic categories:

- Viral/attention topics.
- Trust topics.
- Case topics.
- Myth-busting topics.
- Personal story topics.
- DM-conversion topics.
- Series topics.

## 9. Script And Copy Templates

Include at least 3 reusable templates:

- Contrarian hook.
- Case breakdown.
- Self-introduction/brand story.
- Q&A.
- DM lead magnet.

## 10. Funnel And SOP

Design:

- Profile landing path.
- DM keyword.
- Lead magnet.
- Intake questions.
- Follow-up message sequence.
- Consultation or appointment flow.
- Review/reposting mechanism.

## 11. 90-Day Growth Plan

Month 1: positioning, profile, pinned content, first content pillars.

Month 2: series output, cases, interaction, DM lead magnet.

Month 3: live/Q&A/event/offline conversion, collaborations, review and iteration.

Include weekly posting targets and review metrics.

## 12. KPI

Track:

- Views and completion rate.
- Saves/shares/comments.
- Profile visits.
- Follows.
- DMs.
- Qualified leads.
- Appointments.
- Conversions.
- Referral signals.
