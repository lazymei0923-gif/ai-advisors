# Commercial Funnel

Use this for business accounts, stores, professional services, beauty, nails, makeup purchasing, divination, insurance recruiting, and offline customer acquisition.

## Business Diagnosis

Clarify:

- Offer: what is sold?
- Audience: who buys and why?
- Decision cycle: impulse, considered, or high-trust?
- Trust barrier: price, expertise, risk, aesthetics, safety, results, privacy, convenience.
- Current conversion path: profile, DM, link, booking, store visit, consultation.
- Offline assets: shop, events, staff, customers, cases, testimonials.

## Content Funnel

1. Awareness:
   - Broad pain, desire, trend, myth, mistake.
   - Goal: views and new audience.

2. Consideration:
   - Case, process, comparison, FAQ, behind-the-scenes.
   - Goal: trust and saves.

3. Conversion:
   - Offer, limited consultation, checklist, appointment, DM keyword.
   - Goal: private messages and bookings.

4. Retention/referral:
   - Customer result, aftercare, community, reposts, reviews.
   - Goal: repeat purchase and referrals.

## DM Funnel

Design:

1. Trigger phrase: simple and topic-specific.
2. Auto/manual reply: thank, identify need, deliver lead magnet.
3. Qualification questions: budget/time/location/problem/current status.
4. Recommendation: one next step, not a full lecture.
5. Appointment or consultation: clear booking path.
6. Follow-up: 24h, 3d, 7d messages.

Example:

```
Thanks for messaging "診斷".
先問你三個問題，我才能判斷你現在卡在哪：
1. 你現在主要想解決流量、信任，還是成交？
2. 你目前一週可以穩定拍幾支影片？
3. 你最想吸引哪一類客戶？
```

## Lead Magnets

Good lead magnets:

- Checklist.
- Self-diagnosis form.
- Free first review.
- 7-day topic plan.
- Quote/price guide.
- Before-after case PDF.
- Appointment discount only after trust content, not as the first hook.

## Offline Customer Acquisition

For local businesses, content must connect to place and action:

- Show location and route.
- Show service process.
- Show customer decision questions.
- Show before/after.
- Show owner/staff values.
- Show booking availability.
- Use city/area keywords.
- Create "first visit guide" and "what to ask before booking" content.

## Commercial Account Content Ratio

Default:

- 40% education/awareness.
- 25% case/result/proof.
- 20% persona/process/behind-the-scenes.
- 15% conversion/offer/DM.

Adjust:

- If low trust: increase proof and process.
- If low traffic: increase awareness and contrarian topics.
- If many DMs but few sales: improve qualification and offer.
- If many views but few DMs: improve profile, CTA, and lead magnet.
